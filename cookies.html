Cookies and local storage

Last updated: 31 October 2026

example.com does not use cookies for tracking, analytics or advertising. The only things stored in your browser are there because a feature you use needs them.

  1. What is stored
  2. Why there is no consent banner
  3. Embedded services
  4. More

1. What is stored

PortfolioOS keeps your settings and progress in your browser's local storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). This data never leaves your device. Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set a short-lived security cookie to tell people from bots.

2. Why there is no consent banner

All of the above is strictly necessary for features you ask for (remembering your desktop, the language you chose, protection against attacks), so the law does not require consent for it. You can still switch saving off below or clear it in your browser at any time.

Saved data on this device

Turn on JavaScript to manage saved data here, or clear the site data in your browser settings.

3. Embedded services

When you open a video, song or website inside Internet.exe or Tunes, it is loaded from that service (for example YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, Spotify or Vimeo), which may set its own cookies according to its own policy. Nothing is loaded from them until you open it.
