Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This page explains what personal data example.com processes, why, and what you can do about it. In short: there is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Data is only collected when you write to me or put your nickname on a leaderboard.

  2. Contact form

  5. Who else processes data
  6. Data stored in your browser

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is Your Name, who runs this site privately. Write to you@example.com about anything related to your data.

2. Contact form

When you send a message I receive your name, e-mail address, the subject you chose and the message. The message is delivered to my mailbox by e-mail; it is not stored in a database on the server.

Purpose and legal basis: replying to you and continuing the conversation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in answering messages) and, if you write about a job or a project, taking steps at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

How long: as long as the conversation needs, and afterwards only as long as there may be a reason to come back to it. You can ask me to delete the conversation at any time.

Giving your data is voluntary, but without an e-mail address I cannot reply.

3. Game leaderboards

When you submit a score in PortfolioOS, the nickname you type, the score and the date are saved on the server and shown publicly to everyone. Nothing else is saved with them; please do not use your real name if you do not want to.

Legal basis: your consent, given by submitting the score (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by asking me to remove the entry. Entries also disappear on their own when better scores push them off the list.

4. Security and server logs

To keep the site running and protect it from spam and attacks, your IP address is used briefly: it is held in memory to limit how many messages and scores can be sent in a short time, and checked by Cloudflare Turnstile before a message is accepted. The IP address is not written to the leaderboard or kept with your message.

The web server keeps short technical logs (time, requested address, errors). They contain no message contents, and older entries are overwritten automatically once the log reaches a small size limit.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in the security of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Who else processes data

  • Cloudflare, Inc. delivers the site and protects it from attacks (CDN, tunnel, Turnstile anti-spam check). It handles your IP address and technical connection data.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers and stores the e-mail with your message.
  • GitHub, Inc. When you open project windows in PortfolioOS, your browser loads public repository data directly from the GitHub API, so GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Services you open yourself. Videos, music and pages opened in Internet.exe or Tunes (YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode, Spotify, Vimeo, Wikipedia, the Internet Archive and any site you type in) are loaded only when you choose them and follow those services' own privacy policies.

Some of these companies are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

The site uses no analytics tools, advertising networks, social media pixels or external fonts.

6. Data stored in your browser

PortfolioOS saves your settings, window positions, virtual disk changes and game records in your own browser, so the desktop looks the same next time. This data stays on your device and is not sent to me. Details and a switch to turn it off are on the Cookies page.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict its processing, receive it in a portable format, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting what happened before. To use any of these rights, write to you@example.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes UODO, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

No decisions about you are made automatically and no profiling takes place.

8. Changes

If the way the site handles data changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
